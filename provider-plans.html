Plan FAQ

Common questions about plans & billing

If you're weighing options, these are the questions providers ask us most often before signing up. Still unsure? Talk to our team.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later? +
Yes. You can move between any of the four tiers at any time — instantly upgrade to unlock more features, or downgrade if your needs change. Pricing is pro-rated for the remainder of your billing cycle.
Is there a long-term contract? +
No. All paid plans are month-to-month with no minimum commitment. You can cancel at any time and your listing will revert to a Free directory listing — your reviews and basic info stay live.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and ACH bank transfers. Invoices and payment receipts are available from your provider dashboard at any time.
Do families pay anything to contact me? +
No — ElderCareMatters is always free for families. They search, compare, and contact providers at no cost. Your subscription covers the visibility and tools you receive as a listed provider.
How quickly does my listing go live? +
Free listings are live within minutes of claiming. Paid plans go live within 24 hours after our verification team confirms your business credentials. Premium plans receive priority onboarding and a kickoff call.
