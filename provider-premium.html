About Sunrise Home Care

For over 17 years, Sunrise Home Care has been a trusted name in Dallas elder care. What started as a small family-owned agency has grown into one of the most respected home care providers in the DFW Metroplex — and our values have remained the same.

We believe elder care should feel like family. Every care plan we build starts with a conversation, not a checklist. We take time to understand your loved one's daily routine, personal preferences, medical needs, and personality — and we match them with a caregiver who fits, not just one who's available.

Our Mission: To help older adults live with dignity, comfort, and joy in the place they love most — "home."

Why Families Choose Us:

  • Fully vetted, bonded, and insured caregivers
  • No long-term contracts — flexible, month-to-month care
  • Same-day starts available for urgent care needs
  • Dedicated care coordinator assigned to every family
  • Weekly family updates and 24/7 emergency line
  • Bilingual care staff (English & Spanish)
🕐 Available 4–24 hours/day, 7 days/week, including holidays

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families ask before choosing Sunrise Home Care for their loved one.

How are caregivers screened and vetted? +
Every Sunrise caregiver undergoes a thorough background check, reference verification, and skills assessment before their first placement. All caregivers are fully bonded, insured, and complete ongoing training including dementia care and CPR/AED certification.
Can you start same-day for urgent care needs? +
Yes. Same-day starts are available for urgent situations such as hospital discharges or sudden changes in a loved one's condition. Call (214) 555-0182 directly to speak with a coordinator and we'll do everything we can to arrange immediate coverage.
Do you have bilingual caregivers? +
Yes. Sunrise Home Care has bilingual care staff fluent in English and Spanish. If your loved one is more comfortable speaking Spanish, we'll match them with a caregiver who shares that language.
Do you specialise in dementia or Alzheimer's care? +
Yes. Our founder Maria Valdez is a Certified Dementia Practitioner (CDP), and multiple team members hold advanced dementia care certifications. Care coordinator James Okafor specialises in dementia care transitions and hospital-to-home discharge planning for memory care clients.
Are there long-term contracts? +
No. Sunrise Home Care offers flexible, month-to-month care with no long-term commitment required. You can increase, decrease, or pause services at any time. Our goal is to earn your trust week by week — not lock you into a contract.
