About ComfortCare Plano

ComfortCare Plano is a locally owned home care agency serving families across Collin County. We focus on straightforward, flexible support — whether your parent needs a few hours of help each week or consistent daily care.

Our caregivers are thoroughly screened, reference-checked, and trained before their first placement. We keep things simple: clear hourly rates, no hidden fees, and no long-term contracts.

What We Offer:

  • Personal care (bathing, dressing, grooming)
  • Companion care and social engagement
  • Meal preparation and light housekeeping
  • Medication reminders
  • Transportation to medical appointments
  • Flexible scheduling, from 4 hours/week to full-time

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families commonly ask before getting started with ComfortCare Plano.

How do I get started with ComfortCare Plano? +
Call (972) 555-0241 or use the "Contact ComfortCare" button on this page. A coordinator will reach out within 4 hours to discuss your needs, explain options, and arrange a free in-home consultation if needed.
What are your service hours and availability? +
Caregivers are available 7 days a week with flexible scheduling from as little as 4 hours per week up to full-time live-in care. Holiday coverage is also available.
Do you require a long-term contract? +
No. ComfortCare Plano offers flexible, no-contract arrangements. You can adjust your care schedule or stop services at any time without penalties.
Which cities and zip codes do you serve? +
ComfortCare Plano serves Plano, Frisco, Allen, and McKinney. If you're unsure whether your neighborhood is covered, call (972) 555-0241 and we'll confirm right away — usually in under a minute.
